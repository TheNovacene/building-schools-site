What we do

End-to-end school building — or any single piece of it. Bespoke builds, hands-on training, and on-demand expertise.

Consulting

Vision design

A clear, future-facing model rooted in your values, your context, and your learners — before a single tool is chosen.

Consulting

Infrastructure setup

Decentralised infrastructure your school owns, audits and edits itself — no vendor lock-in. We build your digital estate with you, then show you how to manage it and stay in control.

Consulting

Policy & compliance

Safeguarding, attendance, assessment and SEND policies aligned with Ofsted expectations — compliance as a moat, not a chore.

Consulting

Staffing & curriculum

Educator recruitment and flexible curriculum frameworks that fit hybrid and online delivery — and the humans doing it.

Training

Teacher training & CPD

Practical, hands-on training for teaching teams working online and hybrid — pedagogy, technology, assessment and engagement.

Consulting

Launch & community activation

Public launch guidance, enrolment strategy, and the community-building that turns a provision into a place.

Who we work with

Local authorities and commissioners looking for provision for SEND, EHCP and out-of-school learners. Schools, trusts and alternative provisions building hybrid models. Families and community-led projects who need something the system doesn't offer yet. International schools and organisations building glocal networks.

The pattern is always the same: discovery & needs analysis → design & build → launch & train → support & scale.
