Case Study: The Haven and Verse-al Learning

23 August 2025

Learning DesignSchool Models & Case StudiesVision & Future of Education

Re-enchanting the science of teaching

At The Haven Academy, every lesson is built on a distinctive framework we call verse-ality. This is more than a teaching method: it is our operating system for learning, ensuring that every student — whatever their background, needs, or starting point — can thrive.

Where traditional curricula risk overwhelming or excluding learners, verse-ality creates a consistent, humane structure across subjects. It is rooted in trauma-informed practice, neurodivergent-affirming design, and aligned with Ofsted/ISI frameworks.

The Four Anchors of Verse-ality

  • Relational – Every lesson connects the learner’s mind, body, and world.
  • Symbolic – Knowledge is patterned and meaningful, not just memorised.
  • Recursive – Ideas are revisited and deepened, building mastery over time.
  • Embodied – Wellbeing and regulation are woven into the learning journey.

These anchors make sure that learning is not just effective, but sustainable — supporting confidence, independence, and resilience.

The Five-Part Lesson Template

Every Haven lesson follows the same five-part sequence:

  1. The Knowing World – core concepts and knowledge.
  2. The Human Story – connections to real life, history, culture, or justice.
  3. The Expressive Self – opportunities to articulate, create, and explore voice.
  4. The Designed World – applying knowledge through problem-solving, design, or practical enquiry.
  5. The Living Body – reflective pauses and regulation activities that link learning to wellbeing.

This template runs through all subjects — Maths, English, Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Environmental Science, and Marine Science — providing consistency across the curriculum.

Why It Matters

  • Safeguarding first: Reflection and regulation are built into every lesson.
  • Compliance assured: Directly aligned with Ofsted’s Quality of Education and ISI’s holistic criteria.
  • Equity embedded: Designed with Universal Design for Learning (UDL), affirming neurodivergent strengths.
  • Progression secured: Works at Key Stage 3 for foundations, and at GCSE/Functional Skills for outcomes.

Verse-ality ensures Haven students are not only exam-ready, but also able to think critically, express themselves, and carry learning into life.

A Living Example

In a recent GCSE Science module, a lesson on Newton’s Laws began with The Knowing World — introducing the equations of motion. The Human Story linked these laws to Galileo’s experiments and questions of access to technology. The Expressive Self asked students to sketch or dramatise what “inertia” might look like in their own lives. The Designed World guided them to model force and motion in an online simulation. Finally, The Living Body invited them to pause, notice their breath, and reflect on balance and resistance in their own bodies.

This is verse-ality in action: rigorous, humane, and coherent.

Building Schools in the Cloud

Verse-ality is how The Haven Academy models future-facing pedagogy:

  • Consistent across subjects.
  • Grounded in safeguarding and compliance.
  • Flexible enough for learners who thrive outside conventional classrooms.

This case study demonstrates how Haven is not only preparing students for exams, but also pioneering a new way of teaching in the cloud — structured, inclusive, and alive with meaning.
